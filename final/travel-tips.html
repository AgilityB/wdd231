Travel tips and trip planning

Helpful starting points for a memorable Nigerian adventure.

Before you go

Check official travel advice, current local conditions, and opening arrangements for attractions. Confirm bookings and transport in advance.

Getting around

Allow extra time for road journeys. Use established transport providers and agree on routes and costs before setting off.

Respect local culture

Ask before photographing people or cultural sites, follow site rules, and respect local customs and religious spaces.

Pack thoughtfully

Carry drinking water, sun protection, suitable footwear, a charged phone, and clothing appropriate for the season and destination.
