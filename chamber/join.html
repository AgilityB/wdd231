NP Membership

NP Membership is for non-profit organizations and has no membership fee.

  • Networking with chamber members
  • Community event participation
  • Access to selected training opportunities

Bronze Membership

Bronze is an entry-level paid membership with more business-focused benefits.

  • Networking opportunities
  • Selected chamber events
  • Training opportunities
  • Business directory listing

Silver Membership

Silver provides the Bronze benefits plus additional promotional and event opportunities.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Event discounts
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Featured promotional opportunities

Gold Membership

Gold provides the highest level of benefits and promotional opportunities in this sample membership structure.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Priority networking opportunities
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Business spotlight opportunities on the chamber home page
